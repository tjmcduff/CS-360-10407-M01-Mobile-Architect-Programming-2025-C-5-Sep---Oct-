CS-360-10407-M01 Mobile Architect &amp; Programming 2025 C-5 (Sep - Oct)
Briefly summarize the requirements and goals of the app you developed. What user needs was this app designed to address?
My app was a weight tracking app designed to help users log and monitor their weight changes over time. The goal was to make it easy for people to see progress toward their health goals in a simple and organized way.

What screens and features were necessary to support user needs and produce a user-centered UI for the app? How did your UI designs keep users in mind? Why were your designs successful?
The app included a login screen, a screen for entering weight data, and a screen to view stored entries. I kept the layout clean with easy navigation and readable text. The design was successful because it stayed simple and focused on what users actually needed without extra clutter.

How did you approach the process of coding your app? What techniques or strategies did you use? How could those techniques or strategies be applied in the future?
I coded one part at a time, testing as I went. I used examples from class and Android Studio’s built-in tools to stay organized. In the future, I’ll keep using that step-by-step approach because it makes debugging and adding new features much easier.

How did you test to ensure your code was functional? Why is this process important, and what did it reveal?
I ran the app in the Android emulator and checked that each button and input worked as expected. Testing showed me where small mistakes could cause big issues and helped make the app more stable.

Consider the full app design and development process from initial planning to finalization. Where did you have to innovate to overcome a challenge?
I had to get creative with fixing layout and permission issues, especially with some features not showing up right at first. Adjusting the XML and testing on the emulator helped me find the best setup.

In what specific component of your mobile app were you particularly successful in demonstrating your knowledge, skills, and experience?
I was most successful in connecting the database to the app so user data could be stored and viewed. It showed I could make the app functional beyond just the design part.
